About

Kunjar Bhaduri

Kunjar Bhaduri is a technology executive in Dallas who works with financial-services leaders and private equity sponsors when technology has started to limit the business.

What I do

I'm usually brought in when technology has started to limit the business: a failing program, a client ready to leave, a platform nobody fully understands, or a cyber incident. My first job is to find out what is actually broken and who owns each decision, then set priorities the business can execute.

On this site I write about banking, payments and insurance technology, and about controls for AI agents in regulated businesses: what the system may do, how that limit is tested, and what happens when a control fails. That last question is the subject of the Autonomy Ladder, which I designed and open-sourced.
