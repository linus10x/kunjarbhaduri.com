About

I'm a CTO who works with private equity sponsors and leadership teams in financial services and other regulated businesses. I'm open to permanent, interim and fractional CTO, CIO and Chief AI Officer roles, and to technology operating partner and board work. Recruiters and sponsors can contact me through LinkedIn.

I'm usually brought in when technology has started to limit the business: a failing program, a client ready to leave, a platform nobody fully understands, or a cyber incident. My first job is to find out what is actually broken and who owns each decision, then set priorities the business can execute.
