About

I'm a CTO who works with private equity sponsors and leadership teams in financial services and other regulated businesses. I'm open to permanent, interim and fractional CTO, CIO and Chief AI Officer roles, and to technology operating partner and board work. Recruiters and sponsors can contact me through LinkedIn or book fifteen minutes on Calendly.

I'm usually brought in when technology has started to limit the business: a failing program, a client ready to leave, a platform nobody fully understands, or a cyber incident. Bhaduri Advisory

Strategy, operations and technology for founders.

I have run P&Ls and technology teams in financial services for most of my career. I am now independent, and through Bhaduri Advisory I put that experience to work for founders in Dallas-Fort Worth, as a fractional or interim operator alongside the founder.

What I can do for a founder:

  • Company strategy and go-to-market. Work out who the customer is, how to reach them, and which partners or alliances can help. I can also support you in investor meetings.
  • Running the business day to day. Decide what to source and how to buy it, agree supplier terms, and keep budgets, pricing and weekly priorities clear.
  • Hiring and keeping good people. Decide which leaders and roles the company needs next, help hire them, and set up the management habits that keep them.
  • Technology, product and AI. Review the architecture and AI-written code before growth makes problems costly, and get data and models ready for real customers.

A good fit is a founder working on a real customer problem who wants an experienced operator involved. I am most useful when the problem touches technology, operations and commercial decisions at once.

Software companies building fast with AI

Teams shipping quickly with AI need an experienced look at the architecture, security and delivery before customers or investors look closely. I review the architecture and the AI-written code, and help the team fix what growth would otherwise make costly.

Companies training AI and analytics models

I help teams check their data, test and monitor their models, and get from a promising model to a first customer pilot.

Founders who need a business operator

Strategy, partnerships and alliances, hiring, budgets and supplier choices, as a fractional or interim operator alongside the founder.

How we could work together

Start with a focused review or a defined project; it can grow into an ongoing or interim role.

  • Focused review. Two weeks, fixed fee. One defined question (go-to-market, product architecture, hiring and supplier priorities); ends with a written recommendation and next steps.
  • Fractional retainer. A few hours a week, billed monthly. Ongoing strategy, operating or technology leadership; decisions and ownership agreed up front.
  • Project or interim. Scoped to an outcome: a go-to-market plan, key hires, a supplier strategy, or a product ready to launch.

On terms: Open to equity for the right founder and business. Cash-plus-equity, project fees and monthly retainers also work. Scope, time and terms are agreed per company before starting, and priced once the work is defined.
